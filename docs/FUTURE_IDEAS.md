# Future project ideas — parking lot (not a TODO list)

These are possibilities worth remembering for a later discussion. They are **not approved features, scheduled work, launch requirements, or changes to the current questionnaire**. Keep the current free, offline questionnaire development separate.

## Optional paid psychiatrist report review / consultation

**Idea (9 November 2026):** Keep completing the questionnaire and generating a doctor-ready PDF completely free. As a separate, optional paid service after the core project is finished, a user could choose from participating psychiatrists and explicitly send their report for a professional review or appointment. The doctor would receive most of the fee; the project could receive a disclosed platform/service share, **only if that arrangement is lawful and ethically permitted**.

**Principles to preserve:**
- The free questionnaire and PDF must never be paywalled; users can take their report to any doctor.
- No automatic upload, tracking, server-side storage, or transmission of sensitive questionnaire answers. Any sending requires clear, separate user action and consent; explore a secure direct-to-doctor mechanism that avoids platform access to medical answers.
- Do not promise a diagnosis from the PDF alone. The clinician independently decides what assessment, contact, or appointment is required.
- Before considering implementation, verify Latvian rules on physician referrals, fee-sharing, medical advertising, privacy (including GDPR), and payment handling; consult participating clinicians.

**Status:** Idea only — **not a TODO, not part of the current roadmap**.
