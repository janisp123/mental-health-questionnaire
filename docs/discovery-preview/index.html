Questions to prepare for a mental-health appointment

A free, private, offline, multilingual, non-diagnostic questionnaire for adults preparing to speak with a psychiatrist, therapist or another mental-health professional.

Choose from 712 questions in 15 optional sections, save progress manually, and prepare a PDF summary to discuss with a professional. Available in Latvian, English, Russian and Ukrainian.

Technical-review project: it has not been clinically validated and does not replace medical assessment.

Organise what you want to discuss

This questionnaire is for adults who would like questions to help them reflect on their experiences before an appointment. You can choose relevant sections, skip questions and prepare a partial summary; you do not need to answer all 712 questions.

It does not diagnose, calculate a clinical screening score, interpret your answers, recommend treatment or decide whether you need care. It is not a symptom checker, therapy chatbot or professional endorsement.

How to download and open the offline questionnaire

  1. Open the linked Drive folder and download 187_Questionnaire_FULL_712Q_LV_EN_RU_UK_v0.2.5_REVIEW_BUILD.html. The Drive preview and GitHub source viewer do not run the app.
  2. Open the downloaded .html file in a browser. On a phone, look in Files → Downloads and choose Open with → browser if offered.
  3. Select your language and the sections you want to use. The downloaded questionnaire runs offline.
  4. To continue later, use the app's manual progress-file download, keep the file safely, and import it when you return.

If the HTML appears as code, choose a browser instead of a text/source viewer. If your phone offers no browser for local HTML, use a desktop browser. Physical-phone downloaded-file testing remains pending.

Open the separate PDF guide in a PDF viewer. If the GitHub mobile app does not handle the guide link, open this page in a browser.

Looking for a depression test?

If you want questions to help describe low mood before talking to a professional, this free offline questionnaire can help you prepare. Its depression section asks about loss of interest, low mood, sleep, energy, appetite, concentration, feelings about yourself, changes in movement, thoughts about death or self-harm, and effects on daily life. Many of these questions refer to the past two weeks.

This is an appointment-preparation questionnaire, not a validated depression screening test. It does not give a depression score, diagnose depression or rule it out. If you need a clinical screening test or diagnosis, ask a qualified healthcare professional which assessment is appropriate. You can seek help without completing any test.

What other concerns can I prepare to discuss?

Optional questions also cover anxiety, attention and activity difficulties, social and sensory experiences, changes in mood and energy, sleep, medicines, substance use and safety. Answers are a starting point for discussion; they cannot establish whether you have ADHD, autism, bipolar disorder or another condition.

Four languages in one file

Latvian is the reference and initial language. English, Russian and Ukrainian translations are drafts pending human review. The clinician report language can be chosen independently of the questionnaire language.

Privacy and your PDF summary

The downloaded questionnaire runs on your device and sends no answers. It does not require an account or upload a completed form. This description page has no analytics, forms, scripts or external font requests; visiting hosting and download services still involves those services.

You choose whether to create and share a clinician PDF summary. The project-feedback report is separate. Sending feedback opens your own email app; only saved feedback is included, and you decide whether to send it. Your email service can retain what you send. Progress files, reports and printed copies may contain sensitive information: store them carefully and review them before sharing. Your browser, device, backups or chosen sharing service can retain copies.

Common questions before an appointment

Where can I find questions to prepare for a psychiatrist appointment?

You can download this questionnaire and use whichever sections help you organise what you want to discuss. Bring a summary or your own notes if useful; completing a questionnaire is not a requirement for seeking help.

Is this a good test to find out whether I have depression?

It is designed to organise experiences for an appointment. Its diagnostic accuracy has not been established, so it cannot reliably tell you whether you have depression. The number of questions and a technically working app do not establish clinical accuracy.

Can I use it before seeing a therapist?

Yes. It is a preparation aid for a conversation with a mental-health professional. Your professional decides how, or whether, to use the information in your appointment.

Can I save a PDF for my doctor without completing everything?

Yes. You can skip questions and prepare a report from the answers you choose to provide. The summary records your responses; it does not provide a diagnosis or interpretation.

Do I need to stay online or save an account?

No account is needed. Internet access is needed to obtain the file; the downloaded HTML then runs offline. Download a progress file manually if you want to resume later.

Is it clinically validated or ready for every phone?

No clinical validation is claimed. Human language and clinical review, and testing of downloaded files on physical phones, remain pending. Automated technical checks do not establish those results.

Is there a charge?

Anyone may use the questionnaire for free, including clinicians during appointments. Source and content reuse terms are separate; no general license has been granted.

What if I need help urgently?

This questionnaire is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services. For other support, search for a mental-health helpline or crisis support service in your country or city and check that it is a trusted local service.
