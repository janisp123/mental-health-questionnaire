  - **Purpose:** Evaluate whether this free, offline, non-diagnostic questionnaire helps hesitant users organise experiences and feel more comfortable considering a conversation with a mental-health professional. Assess the *existing* application, not an idealised commercial product; avoid perfectionism and unnecessary features.
  - **1 — User motivation/help-seeking:** Inspect the introduction, navigation, section/completion flow and final report preparation.   - **3 — Completion barriers/usability:** Inspect questionnaire length, optional sections, progress, skipping, backtracking, autoswitch/selection behaviour, save/restore and partial completion; identify realistic abandonment risks, confusing transitions and unnecessary taps.

Avoid repetitive disclaimers or needless infrastructure.
