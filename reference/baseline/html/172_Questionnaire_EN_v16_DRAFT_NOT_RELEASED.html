A questionnaire for adults (18+) to help organize information before a visit to a mental health professional. It is in the development stage; independent professional and language review is still required.

You do not need to answer all 712 questions. After a short introductory information and safety questions, you will be able to choose the topics you need from 48 sections:

  • Help choose — mark what worries you.
  • Sections indicated by the specialist — mark them in a numbered list, for example, 02, 03, 06, 08.
  • Choose for yourself — mark individual sections or all.

First and last name are optional. Additional questions can be skipped. You must select an answer for the initial security questions, including “I don’t want to answer” is valid. You can stop and save unfinished results.

Responses are retained after "Save and Continue". Download the continuation file before you take a break. Responses may remain on your device; they are not automatically sent or monitored.

The questionnaire does not establish a diagnosis and does not replace a specialist assessment. In case of immediate danger, call 112; there is no need to complete the questionnaire.

Select sections

You can fill out the entire questionnaire or only the sections indicated by the doctor. The numbers are fixed: for example, 02, 03, 06, 08. The sections can also be changed later. There are no negative answers in the results of an unselected section.

Sections can be selected at any time. First, the initial information and security answers are filled in (refusal allowed); then the selected additional sections follow.

What worries you?

Mark topics that are relevant to you: related sections will be immediately marked in the list below. This is a selection aid, not a diagnostic test. Manually selected sections will be retained when you uncheck the topic.

Check the sections you want to include and confirm your selection. Gray: not started; yellow: started; green: completed. A blue border indicates the current section. For example, 40/40 with “Skip: 5” means 35 questions answered and 5 intentionally skipped. Refusals and indeterminate answers also count as processed questions; this is not a health score. Conditionally hidden questions are not included in the count. Removing a section does not delete saved answers.

Voluntary use of AI and export opportunities

What to do with the answers?

If you wish, attach the downloaded TXT file to an MI service that accepts files. It contains the answers, question texts, a reference, and instructions for careful interpretation. Separate files are not required.

MI can make mistakes. Its explanation is not a diagnosis and cannot confirm that a mental health problem is not present. This customized questionnaire has not been clinically validated in its entirety. Do not use the questionnaire or AI response for self-diagnosis, medication changes, or to delay seeking professional help.

If you are concerned about the answers or the MI's explanation, consult a mental health professional. Even if the MI does not see a problem, see a doctor if the difficulties persist or interfere with your daily life.

Use of AI is voluntary. Responses contain sensitive health information; check the privacy policy of the service you choose before uploading. The questionnaire itself does not send anything and no one monitors the responses. In case of immediate threat to life or safety, call 112; do not wait for an explanation from the questionnaire or AI.

Usage and privacy

Download this HTML file and open it in your browser. It doesn't require internet, an account, or a password. On your Android device, choose a browser that allows opening a local HTML file and JavaScript.

After each "Save and Continue", the response is written to the local memory of this browser, if available. Simple selections are not saved. A phone call usually does not interrupt the open page, however the browser may close it; a downloaded JSON file is the safest way to continue. This only includes responses that have already been saved.

Some options cannot be selected together. If you change an earlier answer, review the related answers. The backup file preserves previously approved answers.

Additional questions can be skipped. For the initial security questions, you must select an answer to continue with the normal filling, “I do not want to answer” is also valid. The questionnaire can be interrupted and exported incomplete. Omission or refusal does not mean that there are no difficulties.

The current situation may change over time. Please review the security issues before submitting your results. The export time of your results does not update the relevance of old answers.

The survey does not automatically establish network connections or collect usage statistics. When you open a volunteer ChatGPT link, you are communicating with an external service. Shared users of the device can see saved responses. Send files only to those you want to share them with.

Legal information, privacy and project

About the project

Gathering information before a visit to a mental health professional. The questionnaire helps organize self-reported experiences; the professional clarifies the answers and evaluates them within their competence. There are no automatic diagnoses or overall score ratings.

An independent, non-commercial project for adults (18+). It will always be free. If it proves useful, a free app on Google Play or the App Store is planned in the future. This is a single offline questionnaire with optional additional sections, not a clinically validated test.

Legal information and communication

Creator: Jānis Paegle. For contact and suggestions: janis.paegle.email@gmail.com. AI is designed to collect feedback, prepare changes, and respond to emails. Please do not send identifiable health information in feedback.

The survey itself does not send responses and no one automatically monitors them. Saved responses may be in your browser and downloaded files. Privacy cannot be guaranteed on a shared or monitored device. Fill out sensitive questions in a place where you feel safe; you can opt out or stop.

If you report to a professional, the confidentiality of the information and any exceptions are determined by the terms of the relevant service and the law, including obligations in the event of child endangerment. Ask the professional about these limits. A single unwanted thought is not, in itself, evidence of abuse.

The questionnaire is not a substitute for professional assessment and does not call for help. In case of immediate danger, call 112; there is no need to complete the questionnaire.
