What can you do with your answers?

If you wish, you can upload your answers and the matching question and interpretation reference to an AI service that accepts files, for a rough explanation of the experiences you reported and topics to discuss with a psychiatrist. Use the matching language version and reference together.

AI can make mistakes. Its explanation is not a diagnosis and cannot confirm that no mental health problem exists. This custom compilation has not been clinically validated as a whole. Do not use it or an AI response for self-diagnosis, changing medicines or delaying professional care.

If your answers or an AI explanation worry you, consult a doctor or psychiatrist. Even if AI finds no concern, seek medical advice if difficulties persist or interfere with daily life.

AI use is optional. Answers contain sensitive health information; check the service's privacy terms before uploading. The questionnaire sends nothing itself, and nobody monitors answers. For an immediate threat to life or safety, call 112 or your local emergency number; do not wait for the questionnaire or AI.

Use and privacy

Download this HTML file and open it in a browser. No internet, account or password is needed. On Android, use a browser that supports local HTML files and JavaScript.

Each press of “Save & Next” stores the answer in this browser's local storage, if available. Selection alone is not saved. A phone call usually leaves the page open, but the browser may close it; a downloaded JSON backup is the most reliable way to continue. It contains only saved answers.

Some choices cannot be selected together. If you change an earlier answer, review any related answers. Your backup keeps previously saved answers.

You may skip any question or select a refusal option where offered. Skipping does not mean that you have no symptoms.

Your circumstances may change while completing the questionnaire over time. Review safety answers in particular before sharing results. The export time does not make old answers current.

The offline questionnaire makes no automatic network requests and contains no analytics. Opening the optional ChatGPT link contacts an external service. Other device users may see saved answers. Share files only with those you choose.
